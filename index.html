➡️ Excellent guessing! Now let's test your listening. This next game is called "Broken Phone" — and it's as funny as it sounds!
📞 Broken Phone Challenge! Teams form lines. The teacher shows a secret message to the LAST person in each line. They whisper it down the line. The FIRST person says it out loud (or writes it on the board). The team with the most accurate message wins!
📞

Broken Phone Challenge

15 min

🎯 Rules

  • ✅ Whisper once to the next person.
  • ✅ Only English!
  • ❌ No repeating.
  • ❌ No writing until the last person.
  • 🏆 Most accurate message wins the round.

🎭 The 3 Rounds

  • Round 1: Short message (7 words).
  • Round 2: Medium message (11 words).
  • Round 3 (bonus): Long message (14 words) = 2 points!

📨 The Messages (reveal one at a time)

Round 1:

"Someone forgot their compass, so we got lost."

Round 2:

"Everyone was tired, and no one wanted to walk anywhere."

Round 3 (bonus):

"There's nothing to eat, and I can't find anything in my rucksack!"

💬 If you forget the message, say:

"Sorry, can you repeat?" • "I think it was..." • "I don't remember, please say it again."

🔄 Peer-Correction Step

Swap papers with the other group. Use a red pen to circle mistakes (spelling, missing words, etc.). Then return the paper and compare with the correct version on screen.

Reading: An Accident in the Mountains

15 min 📘 p.76
It was the last day of their skiing holiday. Sammy, Hannah and their dad were drinking hot chocolate in a café in the mountains. 'We should go back to the village,' said Dad. 'It's getting late.' 'Can we ski through the forest?' asked Hannah. The children were good at skiing, so he said, 'Yes, but we must be quick.' 'Wait! Help me put my helmet on,' said Sammy, but Hannah was already skiing. 'You're such a baby!' she shouted.

Sammy and Dad were following Hannah when they heard someone. 'Ouch!' They found Hannah sitting in the snow. 'I fell over,' she cried. 'I've hurt my head and I have a pain in my leg.' 'Keep still! You mustn't try to move,' said Sammy. 'I learned first aid at school.' 'We need to get help,' said Dad. He was pressing buttons on his phone, but it didn't work.

'Dad, you should wait here with Hannah. She's had an accident. She needs to keep warm. I'll go and get help,' said Sammy. 'Dad, you should go with him,' said Hannah. 'You don't need to stay with me.' 'No! We mustn't leave Hannah alone,' said Sammy. 'OK, Sammy. Keep safe!' said Dad.

Sammy skied to the village. He told the rescue team about the accident. It was getting dark when Sammy and a man arrived in a snowmobile, which was pulling a rescue sledge. Soon Hannah was back in the village. 'You've broken your leg. You need to rest,' said the doctor. 'Thank you, Sammy!' said Hannah. 'I shouldn't call you a baby.'

🔢 Order the events (1–7)

____ a. Dad and Sammy found Hannah in the snow after the accident.
____ b. They decided to ski back to the village through the forest.
____ c. Sammy offered to get help while Dad stayed with Hannah.
____ d. Hannah thanked her brother and said she was sorry.
____ e. Hannah and her family were at a café in the mountains.
____ f. A rescue team came to take Hannah back to the village.
____ g. Hannah started skiing on her own but she fell over later.
✅ 1=e, 2=b, 3=g, 4=a, 5=c, 6=f, 7=d

➡️ Wonderful! Now let's learn the other grammar from the story: should, must, and need to.
🛡️ Grammar Spotlight: should / must / need to. These three expressions all give advice or rules — but with different strength. Let's see how they're used in the story about Hannah.
🛡️

should / must / need to

12 min

🟢 should – advice or a good idea

"We should go back to the village." → It's a good idea.

"You should wait here with Hannah." → I recommend it.

🟠 must / mustn't – obligation or rule

"We must be quick." → It's necessary.

"You mustn't try to move." → Don't do it!

🔵 need to – it's necessary for someone

"She needs to keep warm." → It's necessary for her.

"You don't need to stay with me." → It isn't necessary.

✏️ Practice: Fill in the blanks in your notebook.

1. Hannah has broken her leg. She needs to rest.
2. It's getting late. We ______________ go back to the village now.
✅ should / must
3. You ______________ leave Hannah alone. She is hurt.
✅ mustn't
4. You ______________ wear a helmet when you ski.
✅ should / must / need to
5. I ______________ go to the shop. We don't have any food.
✅ need to

➡️ What a game! Now let's get creative. Ready to write and act out your own ending to the story?
✏️ Create Your Own Ending! In groups, read the story starter below. Then write a new ending (5–7 sentences). When you finish, act it out for the class!
✏️

Create Your Own Ending

20 min

📖 The Story Starter:

"Sammy and Hannah were skiing when they saw a big dark cave. 'Let's go in!' said Hannah. 'Wait!' said Sammy. 'We don't know what's in there.' But Hannah was already inside. Sammy followed her slowly. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise..."

📝 Instructions

  1. Work in groups of 3–4.
  2. Write an ending of 5–7 sentences.
  3. Use at least 2 indefinite pronouns.
  4. Use at least 1 modal verb (should / must / need to).
  5. Act it out for another group!

⏱️ 10 minutes for writing, 10 minutes for performances.

💡 Ideas to get you started

  • 🐻 What if it was an animal?
  • 💧 What if they found water inside?
  • 🗺️ What if they discovered a secret?
  • 🌪️ What if there was a storm?
  • 😱 What if it was dark and they got lost?

💬 Help Phrases:

"Then they saw..." • "Someone was..." • "Everything was..." • "We must..." • "We should..." • "They need to..."

➡️ Amazing stories! Now for the final activity: let's plan a real trip in Colombia!
🗺️ Trip Guide Mini-Poster! In groups, plan a real trip in Colombia (or any country). Create a mini-poster with the destination, activities, and recommendations. You'll present it to the class!
🗺️

Trip Guide Mini-Poster

15 min

📋 Your Poster Must Include

  • 📍 Destination – Where are you going?
  • 🎯 Activities – What can you do there?
  • 🎒 What to bring – You should / must / need to bring...
  • 💡 Tips – Keep safe, keep warm, keep dry...
  • 🎨 An illustration or emoji scene

⏱️ 10 min to create, 5 min to present.

🌴 Sample: Tayrona National Park

📍 WHERE: Santa Marta, Colombia

🎯 ACTIVITIES: hiking, swimming, camping, birdwatching

🎒 YOU MUST BRING:

  • A tent or shelter
  • A first-aid kit
  • A compass (no phone signal)
  • Water and food for 2 days

💡 TIPS:

  • You should keep your food in a rucksack.
  • You must keep dry — weather can change.
  • Don't leave anything behind. Keep the park clean!

💬 Help Phrases for Your Poster:

"You should..." • "You must..." • "You need to..." • "Don't forget to bring..." • "It's important to..."
